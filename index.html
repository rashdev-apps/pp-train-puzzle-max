🚂 Privacy Policy

Train Puzzle Max • Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Train Puzzle Max (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) is a puzzle game in which players guide trains along tracks to their required paths. The Game is developed and published by an individual solo developer, not a company. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play the Game.

2. Information We Collect

The Game does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

However, the Game displays ads provided by third-party advertising partners. These partners may automatically collect certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • App interaction data, such as ad views and clicks
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

3. How Information Is Used

  • To display ads, which help keep the Game free
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • To keep the Game stable and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Play Services – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Other advertising networks used for ad mediation, if any

We encourage you to review their policies to understand how they handle data.

5. Your Choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • You can stop all data handling by uninstalling the Game.

6. Local Game Data

Game progress, levels, and settings are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Game contains no violence, mature, or inappropriate content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help address it.

8. Data Security

We do not store personal information on our own servers. We rely on reputable third-party providers for ads, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
